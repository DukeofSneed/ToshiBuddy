Import a character pack

A .zip or folder with character.json (what Export makes). Drop it here or choose it.

Tips

  • A sprite sheet is a grid of poses with space between them. Transparent or plain backgrounds work.
  • After importing, the next tab shows every pose numbered row:frame so you can choose which poses play when he walks, talks, sleeps, and so on.
  • Quips in .txt: one line per quip; they play as idle remarks. Use .json for tags (see characters/README.md).
